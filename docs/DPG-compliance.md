# Digital Public Goods Alignment

ForeAct is designed toward the Digital Public Goods Standard.

## Open License

The code is MIT licensed. Outputs can be released under CC-BY where the relevant
government permits publication.

## Relevance To The SDGs

ForeAct supports climate adaptation, disaster-risk reduction and resilient health
and shelter systems in LDCs and SIDS.

## Platform Independence

The MVP runs as a Python CLI and emits plain markdown, CSV and JSON. It does not
depend on a proprietary messaging platform.

## Privacy And Data Protection

ForeAct uses aggregate community data only. It does not process individual names,
phone numbers, medical details or household-level records.

## Do-No-Harm

Recommendations are advisory, confidence-marked and tied to official triggers.
Human authorities review all assumptions before any action is taken.

## Open Standards And Data

Inputs are YAML, CSV and JSON. Outputs are markdown, CSV and JSON, making them
easy to inspect, archive and adapt.
